How it works

From your budget to boxes at the door.

Minute 0

You send your budget and headcount

Through the quote form. Tell us how many Premium, Deluxe and Normal boxes you want.

Within 10–15 minutes

We message you on WhatsApp

You get what will be inside each box, based on your budget.

Day 1–2

You share your logo and letter

We can help write the letter. You approve a sample.

Production

Local makers get to work

Diaries, pens, perfumes and murtis are made, branded and checked.

Delivery

Boxes reach you or your people

One address or many, we arrange it.
