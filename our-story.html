Gift Love

Gift Love is a gifting company built on one idea: a gift should come from people, not factories.

Everything in our hampers is sourced locally and made in the traditional way. Every order we take goes to potters, printers, perfumers and packers who run small businesses. When your company gifts through us, your festive budget helps them too.

Locally sourced

We buy close to home, from people we know by name.

Traditionally made

Hand-finished work, not mass-produced stock.

Supporting small businesses

A big part of every order reaches small makers directly.
